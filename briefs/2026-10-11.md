## What I found

Not research — arithmetic. The cut moved the weight numbers into one line, which triggered LOOP.md's "PRD arithmetic gets rechecked, not retyped" constraint. The sums check out (the nine terms total 100; role + experience = 35), but one claim built on them does not:

> C3 (20) exceeds the entire company + narrative + location + freshness block (20) combined, so among jobs of similar role fit the one with a person always wins.

20 does not exceed 20. This matters more than a word choice because §9.3 says both invariants "should be CI-blocking": a test written from that sentence as `C3 > block` fails against the shipped weight table, and a test written as `>=` tests a claim the prose does not make. "Always wins" was also wrong — a C3 job scoring zero on all four soft terms *ties* a C0 job scoring full marks on them. It doesn't lose, which is the property worth guaranteeing, and §7.2 already breaks ties to the higher connection tier, so the ordering the invariant promises does hold. The fix states the equality, says the tie is the floor, and points at the tiebreak that resolves it.

The equality looks deliberate rather than accidental — a connection tier worth exactly the soft-signal block is a clean way to size it — so I did not touch a weight to make the old sentence true. That would have been a scoring change dressed up as a Sunday cut, and it would have broken the sum to 100.
