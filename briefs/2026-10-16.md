## What I found

The retention question in B-16 was premised on something that turns out to be false, and the arithmetic took two minutes.

`chrome.storage.local` holds **10,485,760 bytes** unless `unlimitedStorage` is declared ([Chrome docs](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/mv2/reference/storage)). I serialised a realistic row of each kind and ran a heavy year through it — 300 submissions, two outcome events each, a brief every morning: **~400 KB, under 4% of the quota, about 25 years to fill it.** So there is no retention rule to write. Any pruning or rollup scheme would cost code and a judgment call in exchange for deleting the only record of last season, which is exactly the record §10.4's behavioural test needs. That branch is closed in the backlog.

The same calculation found the thing that *is* a problem, one section over. `ExtractedJob` carries `descriptionHtml` (§8.4), and a board's HTML description runs tens of KB — at 30 KB each the same 10 MB fills at about **340 jobs**. §8.1 subscribes a *whole board* the moment you open one job at a company, so a dozen boards reaches that in a season, and the failure mode is a `set` that rejects: discovery stopping quietly, which Principle 5 says it must not do. That is B-17, and it is not yours — but B-11's four responses would give me the real description size for free, alongside the three things it already answers.
