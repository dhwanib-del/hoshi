## What I found

Nothing external: this one came out of the document's own rules. The run started on outreach and the first thing it looked for was where outreach *is* recorded, and the answer was §4's outreach tracking — one of the four stores that line says holds current state. Rule 8 forbids computing a count or a rate from exactly that. So §10.2's outreach counts and reply rate, the metrics that carry §1, were in the same position as the tier table rule 8 was written to ban, and had been since 2026-10-09 when rule 8 went in. The section that exists to catch this missed it because rule 8 was written while looking at the tier table and never run back over the metric list above it.

The sharper half is the asymmetry it exposed. Everything else in the log is something Hoshi did: a submission at `mayMarkApplied`, a brief it rendered. Outreach is the one place where the event happens somewhere Hoshi has refused to go, so the log's write-once discipline buys less than it does elsewhere — the row is honest about *when it was written* and only as good as your marking about when the thing happened. That is why the fix is two kinds and three rules rather than one kind: the dates and the denominators need different treatment from the rest of the log, and §9.5 had already solved the same problem for postings.
