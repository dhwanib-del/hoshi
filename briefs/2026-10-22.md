## What I found

Simplify documents this behaviour itself, which makes it better evidence than any review: it saves custom answers — *"The fields below are automatically saved in this browser only"* — and then *"we'll automatically fill in inputs with corresponding labels using these values"*. Label matching, with **no company scoping documented anywhere**. And with its AI setting on, *"Copilot will attempt to autofill unique questions using your profile"*, which is the inference Principle 2 forbids. ([saving custom questions](https://help.simplify.jobs/article/18-saving-custom-questions) · [autofill settings](https://help.simplify.jobs/articles/8686025-manage-autofill-settings-in-the-simplify-extension))

Two things I'm keeping honest about. **Review-before-submit is not a differentiator.** Simplify's own docs say *"You still review the completed application and click the final Submit button yourself."* The difference is scoping and the protected group, and a future run shouldn't claim Principle 1 here. And the premise that free-text is where your remaining per-application minutes go is **tagged, not sourced** — §10.2's median-time metric is the thing that would retire it. I read the category's abandonment evidence and refused it: CareerBuilder's 60%-quit survey publishes no sample size, and Appcast's completion-rate work (500,000 seekers, 30,000+ completions) measures *employers'* conversion across whole forms, which is not your minutes inside one — and neither number would change the requirement at half its size.

**B-20 is the thing I walked into.** Principle 1 says the final submit is yours and §4 says autofill ends at review, so Hoshi never sees a submission land with the employer — yet §10.5 writes the `submission` row *at* `mayMarkApplied`, §10.2 counts applications submitted and a median time *to submit*, and §5.1's ten minutes run to "a submitted application". That is yesterday's outreach asymmetry sitting under the application funnel, unexamined: either `mayMarkApplied` is an observation or it's your assertion, and the document doesn't say. Left for a Friday rather than folded in today, because one change per run and this one deserves its own.
