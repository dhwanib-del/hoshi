## What I found

**The category gets that date for free by reading the page Hoshi refuses.** Teal's contact tracker is filled by its extension — *"Navigate to a connection's profile … Click 'save contact' to add to your Contact Tracker"* — and a contact's work history is *"imported directly from LinkedIn via our Chrome extension"* ([getting started](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/9509581-getting-started-contact-tracker) · [networking CRM](https://www.tealhq.com/tool/networking-crm), the vendor describing its own product, so tagged past what it claims to do). Huntr's contacts are typed into a form and its docs describe no import at all ([Huntr](https://help.huntr.co/en/articles/10089169-contacts-and-documents)) — which is 10-24's finding from the other side: the trackers source no people either, and only the vendor holding a people database claims to.

That route is refused here three times over — Principle 4 registers content scripts against granted *job-site* hosts, §8.2 gives LinkedIn no adapter, and §8.2's judgment is that the account your network lives on is the worst one to spend. Fine. But the consequence had never been written down: **Hoshi's employer field is an assertion with no date, and it decays on its own.** Median tenure for workers aged 25–34 — the cohort a UMSI alum list is made of — is **3.0 years** ([BLS, January 2026](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/tenure.nr0.htm)), so an alum list built off a 2023 graduating class is at the median already wrong, and §8.1 is seeding your watchlist from it while §9.1 pays 20 of 100 for it.

The way I got there is worth keeping, and it's the new constraint in LOOP.md: **a stored fact has a clock even when nothing in the product moves it.** Honesty rule 8 catches a value the *product* mutates after the event (a decaying tier). §9.5 catches a date the *provider* controls (an `updated_at`). Neither covers a value the *world* falsifies while both Hoshi and you leave the record alone. The tell is cheap — a field with no sibling date — and nobody had asked it of this schema.
