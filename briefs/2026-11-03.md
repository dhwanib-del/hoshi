## What I found

Eligibility and authorization are declared hard gates, which means a job that fails either never gets ranked — so every job that *does* get ranked scores 20 and 5 on them. 25 of the 100 points are a constant.

That makes three numbers the document quotes narrower than they read. Connection is 20 of 100 on paper and a **third** of what actually separates two ranked jobs. And the threshold B-15 has been waiting on is a number on 40–100, so **a fixed 70 is the midpoint of the ranked range, not its seventieth percentile** — which is exactly why that branch produces empty mornings on a small watchlist. B-15 has carried that warning as an intuition for a month; it is arithmetic.

The second finding is the one that asks for different behaviour rather than a different description.
