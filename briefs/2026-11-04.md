## What I found

Yesterday afternoon the weight table moved for the first time since September. This morning I went looking for what in the document was still quoting the old one, and found four places. Three were embarrassing rather than dangerous — §9.1 literally gave the gate constant as 25 in one sentence and 40 in the next.

The fourth was load-bearing. **§9.5's freshness bands still capped at 5 points on a term your table pays 10.** A freshness maximum of 5 makes the soft block 5 + 5 + 5 = 15, so §9.3 invariant 2's second clause — the one rewritten yesterday afternoon — read `C3 (15) < 15`. That is false. A test written from the document, in the CI-blocking shape the document asks for, would have failed on a defect in the document. Rechecked both ways in Python: false as it stood, true (`15 < 20`) once the bands follow the table.

This is the 2026-10-11 failure a second time, from the same cause: a weight moved and a figure computed off it did not. So the fix is not four corrections, it is the rule that makes the class impossible — state every term's points as a share of its weight, and let CI assert the relation. I fault-injected it to be sure it bites: freezing the bands back at the old 5-point term fails four tests, three of them the new ones.

Yesterday's lesson was *a weight table is only as live as its least-derived consumer*, learned when `authorization` kept scoring a hardcoded 5 after you moved it to 20. Today it turned out the document is a consumer too.
