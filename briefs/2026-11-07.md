## What I found

I pointed the audit rule at the weight table itself and asked, of each term, which section computes it. Connection has §9.2. Freshness has §9.5. The gates got their three states on Tuesday. **Role — 25 of 100, and 25 of the 45-wide ranked range, which makes it the majority of what separates two ranked jobs — had a weight and nothing else**, for nine runs of §9. The biggest number in the table was the one nobody suspected, because a term's *weight* looks so specified that you don't check whether its *value* is.

The part worth your attention is the 0 band. `unmatched` means Hoshi found none of your saved lanes in the posting's words. That is not the same claim as *a role you cannot do* — but the floor treats them identically, the job leaves the ranked set, and block 3 shows neither a score nor a reason. So the one surface where you could notice a mis-taxonomy was blank. §8.8 settled this exact argument a month ago for listing state — *a failure to observe is not an observation of absence* — and the same sentence was false one domain over, in a score. Titles are where it bites: 178 UX practitioners reported **69 distinct titles**, 42.7% of them occurring fewer than five times ([Zalando's UX job title survey](https://engineering.zalando.com/posts/2017/01/in-search-of-the-perfect-fit--insights-from-the-ux-job-title-survey.html) — self-selected, 2016, EU-weighted, so direction and not measurement).

The competitive half is about the object, not the method. Jobscan's number is *"based on how well your resume matches up with the job description you selected"*, ranks hard skills first and job title third, and comes with a target — *"we recommend aiming for a match rate of at least 75%"* ([its own tutorial](https://www.jobscan.co/jobscan-tutorial)). That measures your résumé's odds of clearing a filter. Hoshi's role term measures whether the role is yours, and says nothing about odds — §9.5's refusal, one term over.
