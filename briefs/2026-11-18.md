## What I found

**The enums of the two funnels were not parallel.** `outcome` has carried `rejected` since 10-16, so the **application** funnel could record a no. The `reply` kind held `replied` and `refer-offered` — both positive — so the **conversation** funnel, the one the thesis runs on, could not. A contact who wrote back *I've left, I can't refer you* had to be stored with the same word as a referral offer.

**Run rather than argued.** A declining reply and a referral offer return the same tier — **C3 at 15 points, the whole connection weight and the largest single award in the term** — the same line and the same stored row, permanently, because C3 did not decay. The brief was worse than the score: *reply waiting* fires at priority 0, so three declines filled block 1 and dropped the morning's one expiring cooling row. Invariant (21)'s own count reported it hidden — the count added on 11-14, firing for a cause that run could not have seen, with the product's three scarcest slots spent on closed doors. The write site had the same defect in miniature: `background.js` wrote `step: data.step || 'replied'`, defaulting the field that carries the answer to the value that scores the top tier.

**What the append-only store allows, and what it does not.** The tier is *derived* from the log, so a row written with the wrong step is repaired by appending the correcting one. But a `submission` already stamped `tier: 'C3'` cannot be reclassified, and nothing marks which of those rows was a decline — the weight table has not moved since 11-03, so not even `weightsVersion` draws a boundary. That part is permanent and is recorded rather than papered over.

**The category records that you spoke, not what came back.** A Teal contact carries *"relationship, goal for connecting, status, and a follow-up date"* plus free notes, with no field for an outreach outcome and no stated values for that status ([Teal](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/9509581-getting-started-contact-tracker), the vendor documenting its own product, so tagged past what it claims to do; the absent value list is a null result, not evidence the values do not exist). What share of replies are declines was deliberately not researched: nothing here changes at half of it.

**One thing this run specified that has no door.** `src/panel.js` records no reply at all — the only writer is a background message. So the document now names three rows Hoshi cannot observe and you have no surface for: a person (B-10), a hand-sent submission (B-21), and now an answer. The answer is the cheapest of the three, since the thread is already in the log and all it needs is which of three words came back, and when.
